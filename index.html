About

Zhang is a Ph.D. Candidate in AI at the University of Ottawa (VIVA Lab), expected around April 2027. He builds physics-informed world models for autonomous driving—and for the harder question of how cars, satellites, and rail sensing can help a country forecast how cities move.

He is a Researcher at the National Research Council Canada (NRC). Recent published work includes FreeOcc (NeurIPS 2026), an IROS 2026 Oral on physics-aware LiDAR densification, and Lunar-ODNet with MDA Space for Canada’s moon rover. Earlier: M.A.Sc. at uOttawa (ICRA 2024 Oral & RA-L), Data Scientist (LLM) at Flipboard in Beijing, and collaborations with NXP, LeddarTech, Sensor Cortek, and Synopsys. B.Sc. from Nanjing University.

He also works with generative AI and multimodal LLMs—fine-tuning, RAG, and LLM-assisted research workflows. Off the keyboard: orchestral music, off-roading, hiking, cross-country skiing. Canadian-certified pilot.
