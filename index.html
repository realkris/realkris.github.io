About

Zhang is a Ph.D. Candidate in AI at the University of Ottawa (VIVA Lab), expected around April 2027. He builds physics-informed world models for autonomous driving—and for the harder question of how cars, satellites, and rail sensing can help a country forecast how cities move.

He is a Researcher at the National Research Council Canada (NRC). Published work includes FreeOcc (NeurIPS 2026), an IROS 2026 Oral on physics-aware LiDAR densification, and Lunar-ODNet with MDA Space for Canada’s moon rover.

Earlier: M.A.Sc. at uOttawa (ICRA 2024 Oral & RA-L), Data Scientist (LLM) at Flipboard in Beijing, and collaborations with Microsoft, NXP, LeddarTech, Sensor Cortek, and Synopsys. He earned his B.Sc. in Computer Science at Nanjing University, and remains fascinated by the field—working to push this great discipline into every industry that needs it.

He is also deeply interested in generative AI and multimodal LLMs—fine-tuning and serving. Since 2020 he has used LLMs to help with research workflows. He thinks carefully about context windows and RAG, and is preparing a submission to EMNLP 2027.

Away from work: orchestral music, off-roading, hiking, cross-country skiing.
