Research

Physics-informed world models for driving—and for cities seen from above.
uOttawa VIVA Lab · NRC · MDA Space

Focus

I work on physics-informed world models and full-stack autonomous driving: perception → forecasting → planning. That means 4D occupancy forecasting, LiDAR densification that respects sensor physics, and national-scale sensing questions at NRC—how vehicles, satellites, and rail can jointly forecast how a city moves.

With MDA Space we built Lunar-ODNet for Canada’s moon rover: lightweight occupancy with high reliability on an internal benchmark.
