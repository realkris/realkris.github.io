Research

Physics-informed world models for driving—and for cities seen from above.
VIVA Lab · NRC · MDA Space

Focus

I work on physics-informed world models and full-stack autonomous driving: perception → forecasting → planning. Lately that means 4D occupancy forecasting, geometry-aware sparse world models, radar-guided ultra-fast forecasters (SSM), and LiDAR densification that respects sensor physics—so free space stays empty of ghost points.

At NRC I also ask a national-scale question: how vehicle sensors, satellites, and rail sensing can jointly forecast how a city moves. With MDA Space we built Lunar-ODNet for Canada’s moon rover— lightweight occupancy with high reliability on an internal benchmark.
